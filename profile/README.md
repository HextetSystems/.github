Hextet Systems is an IT consulting company focused on network infrastructure design, integration, and automation.

## Network expertise
We consult on and operate networks across the full network stack; our expertise includes:
- Layer 1 / Layer 2 / Layer 3
- Mikrotik (RouterOS) design and automation
- FreeRADIUS and custom FreeRADIUS integrations
- DNS design and management
- BGP design, peering, and routing policies

## Tools & releases
We aim to publish tools, scripts, and configurations that are useful for other teams running similar networks.
